Personal sync

Personal sync is optional. It keeps one copy of your planner in your own Google Drive or your own Dropbox, so your devices stay in step. That account is yours, and your agreement with Google or Dropbox applies to it; we don’t run it and never see it.

The copy is sealed on your device with a passphrase you choose before it leaves. We never receive that passphrase and can’t recover it: if you forget it, a sealed copy can’t be opened, so keep a backup of your planner too.
