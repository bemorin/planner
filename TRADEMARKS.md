# Name and logo

Daily Planner's code is open source under the [MIT licence](./LICENSE). This page says what that licence does not cover: the project's name and its logo.

## What the MIT licence covers

The code: the JavaScript, HTML, CSS, scripts, tests and documentation in this repository. You may use, copy, change, publish and share it as the licence allows.

## What it does not cover

The name “Daily Planner by Ben Morin” and the Horizon logo (a sun rising over a horizon line, in a rounded tile) are not licensed for use in forks or derived products.

These files carry the logo and are not covered by the MIT licence:

- `favicon.svg`
- `favicon.ico`
- `apple-touch-icon.png`
- `pwa-192.png`
- `pwa-512.png`
- `welcome/og.png`

The logo is also drawn inline in a few source files. Their code is MIT; the logo drawn in them is not:

- `index.html` (the opening screen)
- `src/features/quarter-print.js` (the printed quarter)
- `src/features/year-review.js` (your year in review)
- `src/features/share-image.js` (the image you can share)
- `src/features/sponsor-moment.js` (the sponsor moment)

## Forks and copies

If you publish a fork, or run a public copy of the planner:

- give it a name of its own, not “Daily Planner” or “Daily Planner by Ben Morin”;
- give it an icon of its own: replace the files above, and the logo drawn in the source files;
- don't present it as the original, or suggest that it is endorsed by the project.

You may say, accurately, that it is based on Daily Planner's code.
