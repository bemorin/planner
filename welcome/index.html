One small loop, every day

Plan in the morning, work from your schedule, close the day in the evening. It takes a few minutes, and nothing unfinished gets lost.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Write one thing to keep in mind, choose the most important outcome and up to two others, then give them time on your schedule.

  2. 2

    Do

    Work from the schedule. Tick outcomes, tasks and time blocks as they get done.

  3. 3

    Close

    Review what got done and decide where every unfinished item goes. Then keep a memory and a lesson from the day.

Your week, in blocks

Week view puts your planning days side by side with their time blocks. It’s the same schedule as the Day view, so nothing needs copying.

  • Pick your planning days: Monday to Friday, or whichever days fit your life.
  • Click an empty slot to add a block, click a block to edit it.
  • Today keeps its current-time line; past days step back.
  • Print a one-page landscape week when you want it on paper.

Yours, on every device

On your computer, your tablet and your phone. No sign-up and no subscription: your plans live in your browser, and you decide where else they go.

  • No account. Open it and start; there’s nothing to sign up for.
  • Works offline. Once loaded, it opens without a connection.
  • Installs as an app on iPhone and iPad, Android phones and tablets, Mac and Windows, straight from the browser. No app store.
  • Personal sync, sealed. Connect your own Google Drive or Dropbox and one file there keeps your devices in step, sealed on your device with a passphrase only you know. Nothing is stored on our servers. Without it, each device keeps its own plans.

The planner

Do I need an account?

No. Open the planner and start. There’s no sign-up, no email and no password.

Is it really free?

Yes, all of it, for as long as you use it: your days and weeks, Horizon, Plan your year, the quarterly check-ins, your year in review, Insights and every palette. There’s no trial, no account and nothing to buy.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser, on your device. It isn’t sent to our servers. You can back up everything to a file at any time and restore it in another browser.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the planner has loaded, it reopens without a connection, which is especially handy when it’s installed as an app.

Is Personal sync safe?

It’s optional, and sealed. Your planner is encrypted on your device with a passphrase only you know before it goes to your cloud, so Google or Dropbox hold a file they can’t read. The planner can only reach its own file there, not your other files, and nothing passes through our servers. Before applying changes from another device, it keeps a few recovery snapshots on your device.

Which clouds can I sync with?

Your own Google Drive or your own Dropbox, whichever you choose in the planner’s settings. Without either, a backup file moves everything from one browser to another.

Can I share my week or my year?

Yes. When you close the last planning day of a week, its summary offers Share your week, and Progress offers Share your year. Either becomes a clean image for a post or a message, made on your device: nothing is uploaded.

Which devices does it work on?

Any recent browser on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac, Windows or Linux. To install it as an app, use Install in Chrome or Edge, or Share → Add to Home Screen on iPhone and iPad.

Open source

Is Daily Planner open source?

Yes. Its code is published under the MIT licence: you can read it, change it and run your own copy. The name “Daily Planner by Ben Morin” and its logo aren’t covered by that licence, so a copy you publish needs a name of its own. The code is on GitHub.

Why is it free?

Because a planner for your own days works best when it asks nothing of you: no account, nothing to buy, and your plans on your device. Daily Planner is an independent project by Ben Morin, with no ads and no data to sell. If it helps you, you can support it on GitHub Sponsors.

Can I run my own copy?

Yes. The planner is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with nothing to build while you work on it. Its README explains how to run it on your computer and how to put your own copy online with Cloudflare Pages.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Send feedback from the planner’s menu, or ask a question on the Contact page. You can also open an issue on GitHub.

Privacy

What this page counts and stores

Your plans are never part of it: they stay in your browser.

Anonymous counts

To learn what helps, this page adds one to a daily count when someone:

    No identifiers, no cookies, no IP address in the counts. To stop abuse, the server limits how often one connection can send it something, counts and forms included. For that alone, it keeps a scrambled form of your IP address, apart from the counts: it can’t be linked back to you once the day changes, and it is deleted within 24 hours.

    Our host, Cloudflare, also measures visits to the site’s pages for us (Cloudflare Web Analytics): without cookies, in totals only. The switch below covers the counts above, not Cloudflare’s measure; a content blocker in your browser stops that one.

    Questions

    A question asked on the Contact page sends only your email, your name and your question. It’s stored only to reply to you and follow up, with your browser’s language, the page version and where the form was opened, and deleted whenever you ask.
